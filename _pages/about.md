<span style="font-weight: 600;">I am on the 2026-2027 job market.</span>

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Economics at Ohio State University. I am an applied microeconomist with research and teaching interests in **labor economics**, **applied econometrics**, **entrepreneurship**, and **household finance**. I study how individuals develop and use human capital to pursue economic opportunities, and how institutions and public policies shape these opportunities. Specifically, I study how workers' skills acquired on the job influence entry into and success in business ownership, how individuals acquire artificial intelligence (AI)-related skills through higher education, and how financial literacy affects financial inclusion. Methodologically, I draw on a wide variety of administrative and survey data, develop novel measures of economic concepts, and use rigorous econometric methods to investigate economic relationships and identify causal effects.
